MOD-01

AI + Web Platform

ScanToSee

A digital menu platform for restaurants and cafés — scan a QR code, browse the menu instantly, no app needed. Businesses get a dashboard to build and edit their own menus, in multiple languages, without touching code. What started as "replace paper menus with a QR code" grew into a full ecosystem.

↳ handwritten menu scanner (CV pipeline)

Image capture → preprocessing → handwriting recognition → text extraction → structured menu data. Explored OCR, PyTorch, TensorFlow, dataset generation, handwriting synthesis. Human handwriting is complicated — especially when everyone writes like they invented their own alphabet.

Agricultural management platform, delivered as both a Symfony web app and a JavaFX desktop app. Five modules: Marketplace, Events, Technicians, Forum, and User Management. Role-based authentication for Admin and Client accounts. Marketplace includes product CRUD and Stripe payment integration.
